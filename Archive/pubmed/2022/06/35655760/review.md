## Risk / unsupported claims
- The claim that AlphaFold 2 predictions agree better with NMR spectral data than structures from standard NMR analysis for one CASP14 target is based on a single cited study and is presented without critical context.
- The statement that "the remaining third of human proteome which is poorly predicted largely corresponds to intrinsically disordered regions" is presented as established fact, but the manuscript acknowledges that a small percentage may be novel folds.
- The claim that NMR spectroscopy can "pave the way for future ML methods" to predict IDP conformations is speculative and not supported by specific evidence.
- The manuscript states that AlphaFold 2 "does not predict the pKa or charged state of titratable residues" and that this is a limitation, but does not discuss whether this is within the intended scope of AlphaFold 2's design.
- The assertion that "the overall performance of AlphaFold 2 on membrane proteins is excellent" is based on a single retrospective study and may not be generalizable.
