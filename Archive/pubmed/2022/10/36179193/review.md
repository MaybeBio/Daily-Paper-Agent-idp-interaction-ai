### Major Concerns

- **Concern ID** R1-M1
- **Severity** Major
- **Blocking** Yes
- **Axis** Evidence sufficiency
- **Claim pointer** The authors claim that "the extent of phase separation varies exponentially with affinity and increases with valency."
- **Evidence pointer** Abstract only; location not provided
- **Concern** The abstract states an exponential dependence of phase separation extent on affinity and a monotonic increase with valency, but no quantitative data, model equations, or experimental measurements are shown to support these functional forms. It is unclear whether the exponential relationship is derived analytically, observed numerically, or fitted to experimental data, and what the relevant parameter ranges are.
- **Why it matters** The exponential dependence is a strong quantitative claim that would be a key result of the paper. Without visible evidence, the reader cannot assess whether this relationship is robust across conditions, whether it is an approximation, or whether it depends on specific model assumptions.
- **Resolution test** The full manuscript should provide the model derivation, the experimental data points, and the fitted or predicted curves showing the exponential relationship. Statistical measures of fit quality and error bars on experimental measurements would be required.

- **Concern ID** R1-M2
- **Severity** Major
- **Blocking** Yes
- **Axis** Claim support
- **Claim pointer** The authors claim that "valency alone determines the symmetry of the phase diagram."
- **Evidence pointer** Abstract only; location not provided
- **Concern** This is a strong and specific claim that valency is the sole determinant of phase diagram symmetry, independent of affinity or other system parameters. The abstract provides no justification for this assertion, such as a symmetry argument, a scaling analysis, or a comparison of phase diagrams with varying affinity at fixed valency.
- **Why it matters** If true, this would be a significant conceptual insight with predictive power. However, the claim as stated is absolute and could be easily overinterpreted. The abstract does not clarify whether this holds for all parameter regimes or only under certain conditions.
- **Resolution test** The full manuscript should include a systematic analysis showing that phase diagram symmetry is invariant under changes in affinity while valency is held fixed, and that changes in valency alter symmetry in a predictable way. A theoretical argument for why affinity does not affect symmetry would also be necessary.
