### Major Concerns

- **Concern ID** R1-M1  
- **Severity** Major  
- **Blocking** Yes  
- **Axis** Experimental support for simultaneous binding  
- **Claim pointer** The authors propose that kinesin-1 and BicD2/dynein interact with Nesprin-2 simultaneously.  
- **Evidence pointer** Abstract, location not provided  
- **Concern** The abstract states that the BicD2-binding site is separated by a ~65 residue intrinsically disordered linker from the kinesin-1 recruitment LEWD motif, and proposes simultaneous interaction. However, no experimental evidence is described that directly tests whether both motors can bind Nesprin-2 at the same time. The abstract mentions binding assays and biophysical studies, but it is unclear whether these include competition or co-binding experiments.  
- **Why it matters** The claim of simultaneous engagement is central to the proposed model of coordinated motor activity. Without direct evidence, the model remains speculative and could be incorrect if the two binding sites are mutually exclusive due to steric or allosteric effects.  
- **Resolution test** Provide experimental data, such as co-immunoprecipitation or fluorescence-based binding assays with both kinesin-1 and BicD2 present, or structural evidence showing simultaneous occupancy of both sites on Nesprin-2.

- **Concern ID** R1-M2  
- **Severity** Major  
- **Blocking** Yes  
- **Axis** Structural model confidence  
- **Claim pointer** The abstract claims a structural model of a minimal Nesprin-2/BicD2 complex with a PAE score in the high confidence range.  
- **Evidence pointer** Abstract, location not provided  
- **Concern** The abstract relies on AlphaFold prediction with a high-confidence PAE score, but no details are provided regarding the specific PAE values, the region of the model covered, or whether the prediction was independently validated by experimental structure determination. The mutagenesis and binding assays are described as validation, but the abstract does not specify how these experiments confirm the structural model rather than merely confirming binding affinity.  
- **Why it matters** AlphaFold predictions, even with high confidence scores, are not definitive structures. The validity of the proposed binding interface and the functional implications depend on the accuracy of the model. Without experimental structural data or detailed validation, the model may be overinterpreted.  
- **Resolution test** Provide PAE plots and confidence metrics, and describe how mutagenesis results map onto the predicted interface. Ideally, include a low-resolution experimental structure (e.g., cryo-EM or SAXS) or cross-linking data to support the model.
