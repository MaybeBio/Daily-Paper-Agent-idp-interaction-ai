The combination of experimental and computational approaches is appropriate for the stated aims. However, the abstract provides only qualitative claims without quantitative support, and the mechanistic distinctions between SK9 and FI10 are asserted rather than demonstrated. The relevance to the broader claim of viral infection exacerbating diabetes is plausible but not established from the provided material.

The work may also appeal to those studying intrinsically disordered proteins and membrane-mediated aggregation mechanisms, as well as computational biophysicists interested in molecular dynamics of amyloid formation.

The use of multiple complementary biophysical techniques and molecular dynamics simulations is a methodological strength. The differential mechanistic proposal for SK9 and FI10, if well supported, would offer novel insight into how viral segments influence amyloid pathways.

### Major Concerns

- **Concern ID** R1-M1  
- **Severity** Major  
- **Blocking** Yes  
- **Axis** Evidence sufficiency  
- **Claim pointer** The study claims that SK9 and FI10 promote hIAPP aggregation in a membrane-mimicking environment, forming distinct aggregate structures.  
- **Evidence pointer** Abstract; location not provided  
- **Concern** The abstract states that SK9 accelerates rapid fibril formation and FI10 stabilizes oligomeric aggregates, but no quantitative data such as aggregation kinetics, fibril yield, oligomer size distribution, or binding affinities are provided. The claim of "distinct aggregate structures" is not supported by any visible experimental output.  
- **Why it matters** Without quantitative evidence, the reader cannot evaluate the magnitude or significance of the observed effects. The mechanistic distinction between the two segments is central to the paper's contribution, and it must be backed by reproducible data.  
- **Resolution test** Provide representative data from circular dichroism, dynamic light scattering, atomic force microscopy, or fluorescence assays showing time-resolved aggregation profiles and structural differences. Include statistical analyses and appropriate controls.

- **Concern ID** R1-M2  
- **Severity** Major  
- **Blocking** Yes  
- **Axis** Mechanistic support  
- **Claim pointer** The study claims that SK9 acts through inter-chain interactions and FI10 through intra-chain contacts.  
- **Evidence pointer** Abstract; location not provided  
- **Concern** The mechanistic explanation for the differential effects is presented as a conclusion, but the abstract does not describe how these interactions were identified or measured. No data from molecular dynamics simulations or mutagenesis studies are shown to support the inter-chain versus intra-chain distinction.  
- **Why it matters** The proposed mechanisms are the core novelty of the study. If not directly supported by simulation or experimental evidence, the claims remain speculative and weaken the overall contribution.  
- **Resolution test** Show simulation snapshots, contact maps, or binding energy analyses that demonstrate the proposed interaction modes. Alternatively, provide experimental evidence such as crosslinking or mutation studies that alter the proposed contacts and affect aggregation outcomes.
