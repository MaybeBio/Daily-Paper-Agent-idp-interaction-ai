### Major Concerns

- **Concern ID** R1-M1
- **Severity** Major
- **Blocking** Yes
- **Axis** Evidence sufficiency
- **Claim pointer** The abstract claims that internucleosomal DNA linker length controls nucleosome arrangement and histone tail interactions, shaping chromatin structure both within and outside condensates.
- **Evidence pointer** Location not provided
- **Concern** The abstract does not specify how linker length was varied, over what range, or how nucleosome arrangement and histone tail interactions were quantified. No quantitative results are presented to support the claimed control of structure by linker length.
- **Why it matters** This is the foundational claim of the manuscript. Without quantitative evidence linking linker length to specific structural changes, the proposed causal chain cannot be evaluated.
- **Resolution test** Provide quantitative data showing distinct nucleosome arrangements and histone tail interaction patterns for at least two linker lengths, with appropriate statistical comparisons.

- **Concern ID** R1-M2
- **Severity** Major
- **Blocking** Yes
- **Axis** Evidence sufficiency
- **Claim pointer** The abstract claims that the balance between intra- and intermolecular interactions governs the molecular network, thermodynamic stability, and material properties of chromatin condensates.
- **Evidence pointer** Location not provided
- **Concern** No data are presented on how the intra- versus intermolecular interaction balance was measured or modulated, nor how this balance correlates with thermodynamic stability or material properties such as viscosity, elasticity, or surface tension.
- **Why it matters** This claim links molecular-scale interactions to mesoscale material behavior, which is a central assertion of the work. Without direct measurements or simulations connecting these levels, the claim remains speculative.
- **Resolution test** Show quantitative relationships between interaction balance (e.g., from simulations or experiments) and measured material properties, with error bars and statistical significance.

- **Concern ID** R1-M3
- **Severity** Major
- **Blocking** Yes
- **Axis** Evidence sufficiency
- **Claim pointer** The abstract claims that mammalian nuclei contain dense clusters of nucleosomes whose non-random organization is mirrored by the reconstituted condensates.
- **Evidence pointer** Location not provided
- **Concern** The abstract does not describe how the comparison between mammalian nuclear organization and reconstituted condensates was performed, what metrics were used, or whether the similarity is quantitative or qualitative.
- **Why it matters** This claim extends the relevance of the in vitro system to native cellular contexts. If the comparison is not rigorously established, the biological implications are overstated.
- **Resolution test** Provide quantitative comparison metrics (e.g., radial distribution functions, clustering statistics) between tomographic data from mammalian nuclei and reconstituted condensates, with appropriate controls.

- **Severity** Minor
- **Axis** Terminology
- **Affected element** "Thermodynamic stability"
- **Evidence pointer** Location not provided
- **Issue** The term "thermodynamic stability" is used without specifying whether it refers to condensate stability against dissolution, free energy of formation, or another metric.
- **Required correction** Define the specific thermodynamic quantity measured or computed, such as free energy of condensate formation or critical concentration.

- **Concern ID** R1-m3
- **Severity** Minor
- **Axis** Completeness
- **Affected element** Methods description
- **Evidence pointer** Location not provided
- **Issue** The abstract does not indicate the resolution of the cryo-electron tomography or the force field used in molecular dynamics simulations.
- **Required correction** Include resolution and force field details in the methods summary to allow preliminary assessment of technical rigor.

## Risk / unsupported claims
- The claim that linker length controls nucleosome arrangement and histone tail interactions is unsupported in the abstract due to absence of quantitative data.
- The claim that interaction balance governs thermodynamic stability and material properties is unsupported without direct measurements.
- The claim that mammalian nuclear organization mirrors reconstituted condensates is unsupported without comparative quantitative analysis.
- The multi-scale structural description from amino acids to network architecture is not verifiable from the abstract alone.
- The causal chain from linker length to material properties is plausible but not established from the provided evidence.
