## Reviewer

However, the evidence base is limited to the abstract, and several claims regarding ease of use, robustness, and sensitivity cannot be evaluated without methodological detail and supporting data. The SnRK1 reporter application is the most concrete claim, but its validation and biological significance remain unclear from the provided text.
- **Who would be interested in the results, and why** Plant cell biologists studying protein interactions, signaling pathways, and post-translational modifications would be the primary audience. Researchers working on kinase activity reporters, particularly for energy sensing pathways such as SnRK1, would also find the approach relevant. The potential for broad applicability across subcellular compartments may attract researchers using imaging-based assays in plant systems.
- **Major strengths** The concept of using phase separation as a readout for PPIs and kinase activities is innovative and could simplify detection compared to traditional methods such as FRET or BiFC. The claim of detecting binary and ternary interactions across multiple subcellular compartments suggests versatility. The application to SnRK1 in stable transgenic plants indicates potential for tissue-specific and dynamic monitoring in vivo.

### Major Concerns

- **Concern ID** R1-M1  
- **Severity** Major  
- **Blocking** Yes  
- **Axis** Technical soundness  
- **Claim pointer** The authors claim "easy detection of inducible, binary and ternary protein-protein interactions among cytoplasmic, nuclear and plasma membrane proteins in plant cells via a robust image-based readout."  
- **Evidence pointer** Abstract only; location not provided  
- **Concern** The abstract provides no experimental evidence, such as representative images, quantification, or controls, to support the claim of easy and robust detection. No details are given on how phase separation is induced, how specificity is ensured, or how false positives are excluded.  
- **Why it matters** Without validation data, the core methodological claim cannot be assessed. Phase separation-based readouts are prone to artifacts from overexpression or non-specific multimerization, and the abstract does not address these risks.  
- **Resolution test** Provide representative images with positive and negative controls, quantitative data on detection efficiency, and a comparison with an established method such as BiFC or FRET.

- **Concern ID** R1-M2  
- **Severity** Major  
- **Blocking** Yes  
- **Axis** Technical soundness  
- **Claim pointer** The authors claim to have developed "an in vivo reporter for SnRK1 kinase activity, allowing us to visualize tissue-specific, dynamic SnRK1 activation upon energy deprivation in stable transgenic Arabidopsis plants."  
- **Evidence pointer** Abstract only; location not provided  
- **Concern** The abstract does not explain how the reporter couples kinase activity to phase separation, nor does it provide data showing that the readout is specific to SnRK1 activity. No information is given on how energy deprivation was applied, how dynamics were measured, or how tissue specificity was determined.  
- **Why it matters** Kinase activity reporters require rigorous validation to distinguish activity from changes in protein abundance or localization. Without such validation, the biological conclusions about SnRK1 activation are unsupported.  
- **Resolution test** Show data demonstrating that the reporter responds specifically to SnRK1 activity, such as loss-of-function or inhibitor controls, and provide time-course imaging data with appropriate quantification.

- **Concern ID** R1-M3  
- **Severity** Major  
- **Blocking** Yes  
- **Axis** Reproducibility and rigor  
- **Claim pointer** The authors state that the SYMPL vector set "lay the foundation for the exploration of PPIs, phosphorylation and other post-translational modifications with unprecedented ease and sensitivity."  
- **Evidence pointer** Abstract only; location not provided  
- **Concern** The claim of "unprecedented ease and sensitivity" is not supported by any comparative data. No benchmarks against existing methods are provided, and the scope of applicability to other post-translational modifications is speculative.  
- **Why it matters** Overstated claims without comparative evidence undermine the credibility of the work and its utility to the community.  
- **Resolution test** Provide quantitative comparisons with existing PPI and kinase activity detection methods, and demonstrate applicability to at least one additional post-translational modification beyond phosphorylation.
