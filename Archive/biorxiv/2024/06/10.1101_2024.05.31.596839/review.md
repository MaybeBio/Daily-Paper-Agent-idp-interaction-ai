## Reviewer
- **Overall assessment** The abstract presents a potentially valuable systematic interaction screen for the Drosophila piRNA pathway, combining experimental and computational approaches. However, the supplied material is insufficient to evaluate the validity, rigor, or significance of the findings. The claims are broad and forward-looking, but the evidence base is entirely absent from the provided scope. The work may interest specialists in piRNA biology and protein interaction networks, but the current abstract alone does not establish the technical soundness or scientific impact claimed.
- **Who would be interested in the results, and why** Researchers studying piRNA biology, transposon silencing, germline development, and protein interaction networks in Drosophila and other animals. The methodological combination of ReLo screening with AlphaFold-Multimer may also appeal to structural biologists and those developing high-throughput interaction and prediction pipelines.
- **Major strengths** The systematic scope across 22 factors is a reasonable approach to map pairwise interactions in a complex pathway. The combination of experimental screening with structural prediction is a modern and potentially powerful strategy. The focus on cytoplasmic factors, including TDRDs and mitochondrial surface proteins, addresses an undercharacterized area.
- **Major Concerns** The abstract provides no quantitative or qualitative results, no validation metrics, and no specific findings. The claim that six complexes were characterized is unverifiable without structural or interaction data. The assertion that the approach will guide future research is speculative and not supported by evidence in the supplied material.
- **Minor Comments** The abstract lacks a clear statement of controls, replicates, or criteria for interaction calling. The choice of 22 factors is not justified. The relationship between the screen results and the six characterized complexes is unclear. The abstract does not specify which complexes were characterized or what novel insights emerged.
- **Technical failings that need to be addressed before the case is established** None can be assessed from the abstract alone, but the absence of any experimental detail precludes evaluation of technical soundness.
- **Assessment against Nature-style criteria** Originality is plausible given the systematic screen and structural prediction combination, but cannot be confirmed. Scientific importance is suggested by the piRNA pathway context, yet the abstract does not demonstrate a conceptual advance. Interdisciplinary readership is possible, but the abstract is too sparse to attract broad interest. Technical soundness is not assessable. Readability for nonspecialists is adequate in prose but lacks context for the significance of the findings.
- **Recommendation posture** Currently not established from the provided evidence. The abstract alone does not support the claims of molecular and structural characterization. A full manuscript with data would be required for any supportive posture.
