A germline KDM3C polymorphism impairs DNA repair and sensitizes to chemoradiotherapy.

Hasan, Adria; Demidova, Elena V; Priyadarshini, Pragya; Czyzewicz, Philip; Gathuka, Leonny; Murayama, Takahiko; Zhou, Yan; Kiss, Zachary Ac; Shastry, Raksha Kr; Andrake, Mark; Hearne, Gavin; Devarajan, Karthik; Wu, Chao; Shah, Anshul; Schultz, Bryant M; Connolly, Denise C; Rosen, Gail L; Canadas, Israel; Liu, Jeffrey C; Burtness, Barbara A; Smith, Jesse Joshua; Dunbrack, Roland L; Golemis, Erica A; Whetstine, Johnathan R; Meyer, Joshua E; Arora, Sanjeevani
medRxiv : the preprint server for health sciences · 2026-09-13 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文研究KDM3C无序区域中的SNP通过AlphaFold预测影响与RNF8的互作，进而损害DNA修复并影响放化疗敏感性，虽对象为IDR且涉及PPI和AI建模，但核心是SNP功能而非互作机制建模，可迁移性中等。

摘要

Chemoradiotherapy (CRT) is the standard-of-care therapy for many solid malignancies, yet predictive biomarkers of treatment response remain limited. We identified a germline single nucleotide polymorphism (SNP) in an intrinsically disordered region of the lysine demethylase KDM3C/JMJD1C (p.S464T) that is associated with CRT outcomes in locally advanced rectal cancers (LARC) and head and neck squamous cell carcinoma (LA-HNSCC). In silico modeling with AlphaFold predicted S464T substitution influenced interaction between phosphorylated KDM3C and RNF8 FHA domain. In cellular models, conversion of S464 to T464 increased sensitivity to DNA-damaging agents. S464T substitution impaired damage-induced MDC1-RAP80 signaling and downstream RAP80-BRCA1 colocalization. SNP carrying cells impaired DNA repair causing genotoxic stress that is associated with increased cGAS-cGAMP innate immune signaling and increased apoptosis. Population analyses with the SNP highlighted an increase incidence of UV-induced skin and other cancers, linking inherited variation in the chromatin regulatory gene KDM3C to genome instability, cancer risk, and therapeutic vulnerability.

摘要翻译

放化疗（CRT）是多种实体恶性肿瘤的标准治疗方案，但治疗反应的预测性生物标志物仍然有限。我们在赖氨酸去甲基化酶KDM3C/JMJD1C（p.S464T）的一个内在无序区域中鉴定出一个与局部晚期直肠癌（LARC）和头颈部鳞状细胞癌（LA-HNSCC）的CRT结局相关的种系单核苷酸多态性（SNP）。利用AlphaFold进行的计算机模拟预测表明，S464T替换影响了磷酸化KDM3C与RNF8 FHA结构域之间的相互作用。在细胞模型中，S464到T464的转换增加了对DNA损伤药物的敏感性。S464T替换损害了损伤诱导的MDC1-RAP80信号传导及下游RAP80-BRCA1共定位。携带SNP的细胞DNA修复受损，导致基因毒性应激，这与cGAS-cGAMP先天免疫信号增强和细胞凋亡增加相关。对该SNP的人群分析突显了紫外线诱导的皮肤癌及其他癌症发病率的增加，将染色质调控基因KDM3C的遗传变异与基因组不稳定性、癌症风险及治疗脆弱性联系起来。

02 一句话总结

本文发现 KDM3C 基因 IDR 区的一个种系 SNP（p.S464T）通过改变磷酸化 KDM3C 与 RNF8 FHA 结构域的互作，削弱 MDC1-RAP80-BRCA1 信号轴，导致 DNA 修复缺陷、基因组不稳定、cGAS 先天免疫激活和细胞凋亡增加，从而与直肠癌和头颈癌患者对化学放疗的敏感性相关。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：种系遗传变异能否作为化学放疗（CRT）反应的预测性生物标志物？具体而言，KDM3C 基因 IDR 区的一个 SNP（p.S464T）是否通过影响 DNA 修复机制来改变 CRT 敏感性？
  • 为什么重要：CRT 是多种实体瘤的标准治疗，但缺乏可靠的预测性生物标志物。若能识别种系 SNP 作为 CRT 敏感性标志物，可指导个体化治疗决策，避免无效治疗及其毒性。
  • 现有方法为何不足：现有 CRT 预测标志物多为体细胞突变或表达谱，缺乏对种系遗传变异的系统评估；且多数研究未深入机制层面，无法解释变异如何影响治疗反应。
  • 精确研究问题：Can a germline SNP in the IDR of KDM3C (p.S464T) impair DNA repair via disrupting phospho-KDM3C–RNF8 interaction, thereby sensitizing tumors to chemoradiotherapy?

06 核心思想

1. 表面方法： - 临床队列关联分析（LARC 和 LA-HNSCC 患者中 KDM3C p.S464T 与 CRT 结局的关联） - AlphaFold 结构预测（评估 S464T 对磷酸化 KDM3C–RNF8 FHA 互作的影响） - 细胞模型功能验证（DNA 损伤剂敏感性、MDC1-RAP80 信号、RAP80-BRCA1 共定位、cGAS 信号、凋亡） - 人群遗传学分析（SNP 携带者中 UV 相关皮肤癌及其他癌症发病率）

2. 核心洞察： - IDR 中的单氨基酸变异（S→T）可以改变磷酸化依赖的蛋白互作特异性，即使该区域缺乏稳定三维结构 - 磷酸化 KDM3C 与 RNF8 FHA 结构域的互作是 DNA 损伤应答中 MDC1-RAP80 信号轴的上游调控节点 - 种系 SNP 通过削弱 DNA 修复能力，不仅增加 CRT 敏感性（治疗机会），也增加癌症易感性（风险）

3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]： - IDR 中的 SNP 不应被默认为「中性」或「无功能」变异；磷酸化位点附近的氨基酸替换可能通过改变激酶底物识别或磷酸化依赖互作而产生显著功能后果 - AlphaFold 等结构预测工具可用于生成 IDR 变异的功能假设，但必须结合实验验证 - 「治疗敏感性」与「癌症易感性」是同一生物学缺陷（DNA 修复受损）的两面，这一概念可推广至其他 DNA 修复基因的 IDR 变异

07 方法总览

  • 输入：临床队列基因型数据（LARC、LA-HNSCC 患者）、KDM3C 蛋白序列、SNP 信息（p.S464T）

  • 模块： 1. 临床关联分析（SNP 分型与 CRT 反应/生存关联） 2. AlphaFold 结构预测（野生型 vs S464T 磷酸化 KDM3C 与 RNF8 FHA 的互作） 3. 细胞实验（CRISPR 编辑或过表达系统引入 S/T 变异；DNA 损伤剂处理；信号通路检测） 4. 人群遗传学分析（SNP 携带者癌症发病率）
  • 训练：不适用（无机器学习模型训练）

  • 反馈回路：AlphaFold 预测 → 细胞实验验证/修正 → 人群数据支持
  • 假设：IDR 中 S464 的磷酸化是 KDM3C 与 RNF8 互作所必需；S→T 替换改变磷酸化状态或磷酸化依赖的互作亲和力

14 Agent 提炼的知识候选

面向课题方向（IDP/IDR × 蛋白互作 × AI/物理模拟）的可迁移知识：

  1. IDR 内 SNP 的功能评估框架：本文展示了「临床关联发现 → 结构预测 → 细胞验证 → 人群支持」的完整流程。可迁移至 IDP/IDR 介导互作的研究：对 IDR 内 SNP 不应默认中性，而应系统评估其对磷酸化依赖互作的影响。

  2. 磷酸化位点附近的氨基酸替换策略：S→T 替换保留了羟基（可磷酸化）但改变侧链长度和构象灵活性。这一「保守但非中性」的替换设计思路可用于研究 IDR 中磷酸化开关机制。

  3. AlphaFold 用于 IDR 互作预测的边界：本文用 AlphaFold 预测磷酸化 KDM3C 与 RNF8 FHA 的互作，说明 AlphaFold 可用于生成 IDR 互作假设，但需实验验证。可迁移：对 IDR 介导互作，AlphaFold 预测应作为假设生成而非结论。

  4. DNA 损伤修复中的 IDR 介导互作网络：KDM3C（IDR 含磷酸化位点）→ RNF8（FHA 结构域识别磷酸化）→ MDC1-RAP80 → BRCA1 的级联，展示了 IDR 磷酸化修饰如何作为互作开关。可迁移至其他 DNA 修复蛋白的 IDR 分析。

  5. 「治疗敏感性」与「癌症易感性」的双面性概念：同一 DNA 修复缺陷既增加 CRT 敏感性（治疗窗口）又增加癌症风险（遗传风险）。这一概念可推广至其他 IDR 介导 DNA 修复的基因变异研究。

  6. cGAS-STING 作为 DNA 修复缺陷的下游读出：修复缺陷→胞质 DNA 积累→cGAS 激活→免疫信号→凋亡。可作为 IDR 变异功能验证的通用下游指标。

Review setup

  • Input scope Abstract only
  • Assessment boundary Claims and evidence as presented in the abstract; no full-text methods, figures, or supplementary data were provided for evaluation
  • Shared manuscript claim summary The authors report identification of a germline SNP (p.S464T) in the intrinsically disordered region of KDM3C/JMJD1C, associated with chemoradiotherapy outcomes in LARC and LA-HNSCC. They propose that this substitution alters KDM3C phosphorylation-dependent interaction with the RNF8 FHA domain, impairs MDC1-RAP80 signaling and RAP80-BRCA1 colocalization, increases sensitivity to DNA-damaging agents, elevates cGAS-cGAMP innate immune signaling and apoptosis, and is associated with increased incidence of UV-induced skin and other cancers in population analyses.
  • Visible evidence base Abstract text only; no figures, tables, methods, statistical details, cohort descriptions, or supplementary materials were supplied
  • Missing materials affecting confidence Full manuscript, cohort demographics and sample sizes, genotyping and phenotyping methods, statistical models and effect sizes, cellular assay details, AlphaFold modeling parameters, population analysis design and covariates, and all primary data

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a potentially interesting hypothesis linking a germline variant in a chromatin regulator to DNA repair impairment and therapeutic response. However, the claims span multiple levels of evidence, from clinical association to molecular mechanism to population risk, and none of these can be adequately evaluated from the abstract alone. The clinical association is stated without effect sizes or cohort details, the mechanistic claims lack experimental validation specifics, and the population analysis is presented without methodological context. The work may be of interest to the DNA repair and cancer genomics communities, but the current evidence base is insufficient to establish the central claims.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers in DNA repair and genome stability, cancer genomics and precision oncology, chromatin biology, and immunology of the cGAS-STING pathway would be interested. The potential for a germline biomarker predicting chemoradiotherapy response has direct translational relevance for oncology clinicians and trial designers.
  • Major strengths The abstract identifies a novel germline variant in a chromatin regulatory gene with potential clinical relevance, which is a relatively underexplored area compared to somatic mutations. The proposed mechanistic link from a single amino acid substitution to impaired DNA repair signaling and innate immune activation is coherent and testable. The inclusion of multiple cancer types and population-level cancer incidence data suggests an attempt at broad validation.
