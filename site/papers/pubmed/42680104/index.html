Multicomplex Integrative Structural Modeling of a Human Histone Deacetylase Interactome.

Nde, Jules; Majila, Kartik; Zimmermann, Rosalyn C; Kempf, Cassandra; Zhang, Ying; Cesare, Joseph; Thornton, Janet L; Workman, Jerry L; Florens, Laurence; Viswanath, Shruthi; Washburn, Michael P
Molecular & cellular proteomics : MCP · 2026-09-01 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文结合XL-MS与AlphaFold的整合建模，构建含HDAC1 CTD无序区域的NuRD等复合物结构，揭示IDR折叠为α螺旋并参与PPI组装，直接契合无序蛋白互作机制建模主题。

摘要

Histone Deacetylase (HDAC) 1 and 2 are key enzymatic components in multiple large chromatin remodeling complexes including NuRD, SIN3, and CoREST. In addition, both HDAC 1 and 2 contain a large intrinsically disordered region (IDR) within their C-terminal domain (CTD). How HDAC1/2 assemble into these complexes and the structure of the CTD IDR remains poorly understood. Here, we used HDAC1/2 to isolate their protein interaction networks from cells and used crosslinking mass spectrometry (XL-MS) coupled with the Integrative Modeling Platform to build structural models of the NuRD, SIN3A, and CoREST complexes. Next, we implemented an AlphaFold-enabled XL-MS constrained modeling approach to investigate how HDAC1 could assemble into these complexes. We show that the CTD IDR of HDAC1 folds into alpha helices in these complexes. Finally, we built a complete integrative structural model of a NuRD subcomplex including the abundant HDAC1:MBD3:MTA1:GATAD2B:RBBP4 subunits, which included 6 IDRs. The approaches used herein are broadly applicable for the study of protein complexes and protein interaction networks that can provide important insights into IDRs.

摘要翻译

组蛋白去乙酰化酶（HDAC）1和2是多个大型染色质重塑复合物（包括NuRD、SIN3和CoREST）中的关键酶组分。此外，HDAC1和2在其C端结构域（CTD）内均含有一个大的固有无序区域（IDR）。HDAC1/2如何组装成这些复合物以及CTD IDR的结构仍知之甚少。在此，我们利用HDAC1/2从细胞中分离其蛋白质相互作用网络，并结合交联质谱（XL-MS）与整合建模平台构建了NuRD、SIN3A和CoREST复合物的结构模型。接下来，我们采用了一种AlphaFold增强的XL-MS约束建模方法，以研究HDAC1如何组装到这些复合物中。我们表明，HDAC1的CTD IDR在这些复合物中折叠成α螺旋。最后，我们构建了一个NuRD亚复合物的完整整合结构模型，包括丰富的HDAC1:MBD3:MTA1:GATAD2B:RBBP4亚基，其中包含6个IDR。本文所采用的方法广泛适用于蛋白质复合物和蛋白质相互作用网络的研究，可为IDR提供重要见解。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：HDAC1/2 如何组装进入 NuRD、SIN3A、CoREST 等大型染色质重塑复合物？其 C 端 IDR 在这些复合物中的结构状态是什么？
  • 为什么重要：HDAC1/2 是多种关键染色质调控复合物的酶活核心，其 IDR 的结构状态直接影响复合物组装机制和功能调控的理解。IDR 在复合物环境中的折叠行为是 IDP/IDR 领域核心未解问题。
  • 现有方法为何不足：传统结构生物学方法（X-ray、cryo-EM）难以捕获含长 IDR 的复合物构象；单独的 AlphaFold 预测对 IDR 区域置信度低；单独的 XL-MS 无法提供三维结构信息。
  • 精确研究问题：Can XL-MS 约束的整合建模与 AlphaFold 预测相结合，确定 HDAC1/2 互作复合物中 IDR 的结构状态及亚基组装模式？

06 核心思想

1) 表面方法

用 HDAC1/2 抗体从细胞中免疫沉淀其互作网络，经 XL-MS 获取亚基间和亚基内交联距离约束，使用 Integrative Modeling Platform 构建复合物框架模型，再以 AlphaFold 预测的亚基结构为参考，将 XL-MS 约束作为距离惩罚项进行约束建模，最终整合出复合物结构模型。

2) 核心洞察

IDR 在复合物环境中可以折叠为 α-helix——HDAC1 的 CTD IDR 在 NuRD/SIN3A/CoREST 复合物中并非保持无规卷曲状态，而是在蛋白互作界面诱导下形成二级结构。这一发现支持「折叠后结合（folding-upon-binding）」模型在大型染色质复合物中的普遍性。

3) 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]

  • 实验约束 + AI 预测的互补性：AlphaFold 对 IDR 的预测不可靠，但实验约束（XL-MS）可以提供 IDR 在特定复合物环境中的构象信息，二者结合可突破各自局限。
  • IDR 的功能结构状态需在复合物语境中研究：单独研究 IDR 无法揭示其功能构象，必须在其互作网络/复合物环境中考察。
  • 整合建模是处理多 IDR 复合物的可行路径：当系统复杂到单一方法无法解决时，多源数据融合是务实策略。

07 方法总览

  • 输入：HDAC1/2 免疫沉淀样本、XL-MS 交联距离约束、AlphaFold 预测的亚基结构
  • 输出：NuRD、SIN3A、CoREST 复合物的整合结构模型，以及含 6 个 IDR 的 NuRD 亚复合物完整模型
  • 模块： 1. 免疫沉淀（IP）获取互作网络 2. XL-MS 交联与质谱鉴定 3. Integrative Modeling Platform（IMP）建模 4. AlphaFold 结构预测 5. AlphaFold-enabled XL-MS 约束建模
  • 训练：不涉及传统机器学习训练；AlphaFold 为预训练模型，IMP 为优化过程
  • 工具：XL-MS（具体交联剂未提供）、Integrative Modeling Platform、AlphaFold
  • 反馈回路：XL-MS 约束 → IMP 建模 → AlphaFold 结构参考 → 约束建模 → 模型验证（通过交联距离满足度评估）
  • 假设：交联距离约束反映溶液态构象；AlphaFold 预测的折叠域结构在复合物中保持

文字流程： 1. 从细胞中通过 HDAC1/2 抗体 IP 富集互作复合物 2. 对富集样本进行 XL-MS 交联处理，鉴定交联肽段，获得亚基间/亚基内距离约束 3. 使用 IMP 基于 XL-MS 约束构建复合物粗粒度框架模型 4. 对每个亚基运行 AlphaFold 预测结构，识别折叠域与 IDR 5. 将 AlphaFold 预测结构作为刚性体，以 XL-MS 约束为距离惩罚，进行约束建模优化 6. 评估模型与交联约束的一致性，迭代优化 7. 对 NuRD 亚复合物进行完整整合建模，包含 6 个 IDR

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围：本文仅针对 HDAC1/2 互作网络中的三个复合物（NuRD、SIN3A、CoREST），不涵盖所有 HDAC 家族或所有染色质复合物。
  • oracle/真值输入：AlphaFold 预测结构作为参考输入，其准确性直接影响最终模型；XL-MS 约束为实验真值，但受交联效率与覆盖率限制。
  • 端到端状态：方法为「半端到端」——AlphaFold 为预训练模型，IMP 为独立优化，二者通过约束耦合，并非统一训练的端到端框架。
  • 算力成本：未提供。
  • 历史数据依赖：AlphaFold 依赖训练集（PDB 结构），对未见过的复合物构象可能失效。
  • 模型依赖：最终模型质量依赖 AlphaFold 预测准确性、XL-MS 覆盖率、IMP 采样充分性。
  • 最难情形：多 IDR 协同折叠、动态构象交换、低丰度复合物。
  • 群体/领域边界：结论基于人类细胞 HDAC1/2 网络，推广到其他物种或 IDR 系统需谨慎。
  • 不确定性：IDR 折叠为 α-helix 的结论基于模型，缺乏独立实验验证（如 NMR、CD 光谱）。

有边界的复述：在 HDAC1/2 免疫沉淀的互作网络中，通过 XL-MS 约束的整合建模，可以构建 NuRD、SIN3A、CoREST 复合物的结构模型，并预测 HDAC1 CTD IDR 在这些复合物中折叠为 α-helix；该方法可扩展至含多个 IDR 的复合物体系，但结论的普适性需更多独立验证。

14 Agent 提炼的知识候选

面向课题方向（IDP/IDR × 蛋白互作 × AI/物理模拟）的可迁移知识：

  1. XL-MS 约束 + AlphaFold 的混合建模策略：当 AlphaFold 对 IDR 预测不可靠时，用 XL-MS 距离约束作为实验惩罚项，引导 IDR 在复合物环境中折叠。可迁移到其他 IDR 介导的互作体系（如转录因子、信号蛋白的 IDR 互作）。

  2. 「复合物环境诱导 IDR 折叠」的检测范式：通过比较游离 IDR 与复合物中 IDR 的结构状态差异，可以系统筛选「折叠后结合」型 IDR。可迁移到相分离研究——检查 IDR 在凝聚体中的构象变化。

  3. 多 IDR 复合物的分层建模流程：先 IP 富集 → XL-MS 获取约束 → IMP 粗粒度建模 → AF 细化 → 约束优化。此流程可迁移到其他多亚基含 IDR 复合物。

  4. IDR 折叠为 α-helix 的生物学意义：HDAC1 CTD IDR 在复合物中折叠为 α-helix，提示 IDR 的二级结构倾向（如 α-helix 倾向性）可能预测其在复合物中的折叠行为。可结合序列预测工具（如 IUPred、AlphaFold pLDDT）预筛 IDR 的折叠潜力。

  5. 免疫沉淀 + XL-MS 的互作组学工作流：从内源水平捕获互作网络，避免过表达假象，适合研究内源 IDR 互作。

15 与已有知识连接

  • 相似方法：Integrative Modeling Platform（IMP）已被广泛用于核孔复合物、核糖体组装等体系（Rout & Sali, 2019）；本文将其扩展至 HDAC 互作组。
  • 相似结论：IDR 折叠后结合（folding-upon-binding）已在 p53、p27 等体系中被广泛报道（Wright & Dyson, 2015）；本文将该现象扩展至大型染色质复合物。
  • AlphaFold 与 IDR：已知 AlphaFold 对 IDR 预测 pLDDT 低（Tunyasuvunakool et al., 2021）；本文通过实验约束弥补此缺陷，与「AF 后处理」方向一致。
  • NuRD 复合物结构：已有 cryo-EM 研究解析了 NuRD 部分结构（如 Low et al., 2020; Zhang et al., 2020）；本文模型可与这些结构对比验证。
  • 相分离连接 [Analysis]：HDAC1/2 的 IDR 折叠行为与相分离中 IDR 的构象变化相关——相分离中 IDR 可能部分折叠形成互作网络。本文方法可迁移至凝聚体结构研究。

候选 1：IDR 折叠倾向性预测器

  • 核心假设：IDR 的序列特征（疏水模式、螺旋倾向性、互作伙伴的互补表面）可预测其在复合物中的折叠行为

  • 核心假设：AF-multimer 在加入 XL-MS 约束后，可预测含多个 IDR 的复合物结构

  • 相对本文的增量：从静态到动态

  • 验证方式：与 XL-MS 交联距离分布对比

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a potentially interesting and technically ambitious approach to studying the structure of large, dynamic chromatin remodeling complexes, with a focus on the role of intrinsically disordered regions. The combination of XL-MS and integrative modeling is timely, and the claim that the HDAC1 CTD IDR adopts alpha-helical conformations in complex contexts is noteworthy. However, the abstract alone provides insufficient evidence to evaluate the validity, rigor, or novelty of the work. The core claims are stated without any quantitative support, and the absence of any structural validation metrics or comparison to existing models is a significant concern. The manuscript may be of interest to the structural biology and chromatin fields, but the current evidence base is too thin to assess its suitability for a high-impact venue.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Structural biologists and biochemists studying chromatin remodeling complexes, particularly the NuRD, SIN3, and CoREST complexes. Researchers interested in the structural biology of intrinsically disordered regions (IDRs) and their folding upon complex assembly. The integrative modeling methodology may also be of interest to computational biologists developing hybrid structural biology approaches.
  • Major strengths 1. The focus on large, multi-subunit complexes with significant IDR content addresses a challenging and important problem in structural biology. 2. The combination of XL-MS with AlphaFold-enabled modeling is a contemporary and potentially powerful approach. 3. The claim of modeling a subcomplex with six IDRs is ambitious and, if validated, would represent a significant technical achievement.

  • Major Concerns

    • Concern ID R1-M1
    • Severity Major
    • Blocking Yes
    • Axis Evidence sufficiency
    • Claim pointer "We show that the CTD IDR of HDAC1 folds into alpha helices in these complexes."
    • Evidence pointer Abstract; location not provided
    • Concern The abstract provides no quantitative evidence for this claim. There is no mention of the number of crosslinks supporting the alpha-helical conformation, the confidence of the secondary structure prediction, or any validation metrics (e.g., crosslink satisfaction rate, model precision, or comparison to known structures). The claim is stated as a conclusion without any supporting data.
    • Why it matters This is a central and novel claim of the work. Without evidence, the reader cannot assess whether the observed alpha-helical folding is a robust, reproducible finding or an artifact of the modeling procedure. The structural state of IDRs in complexes is a key biological question, and unsupported claims undermine the manuscript's credibility.

    • Resolution test The authors must provide, in the full manuscript, clear evidence for the alpha-helical folding of the HDAC1 CTD IDR. This should include: (a) the number and distribution of crosslinks that are consistent with an alpha-helical model, (b) the confidence scores from the AlphaFold or other secondary structure prediction, (c) a comparison of the model to a random coil or alternative secondary structure models, and (d) validation metrics such as crosslink satisfaction rates and model precision for the IDR region.

    • Concern ID R1-M2

    • Severity Major
    • Blocking Yes
    • Axis Evidence sufficiency
    • Claim pointer "we built a complete integrative structural model of a NuRD subcomplex including the abundant HDAC1:MBD3:MTA1:GATAD2B:RBBP4 subunits, which included 6 IDRs."
    • Evidence pointer Abstract; location not provided
    • Concern The abstract does not provide any metrics for the quality or completeness of this integrative model. Terms like "complete" are undefined. There is no mention of model precision, crosslink satisfaction rate, sampling convergence, or any other standard validation metric for integrative structural models. The number of IDRs (6) is stated, but their lengths, the number of crosslinks within them, and the confidence of their modeled conformations are not described.
    • Why it matters Integrative modeling produces ensembles of models, and the quality of the result is critically dependent on the density and quality of the experimental restraints. Without validation metrics, the reader cannot distinguish between a high-confidence, well-constrained model and a low-resolution, poorly defined model. The claim of a "complete" model is misleading without such context.

    • Resolution test The authors must provide, in the full manuscript, standard validation metrics for the integrative model. This should include: (a) the precision of the model (e.g., RMSD of the ensemble), (b) the crosslink satisfaction rate (fraction of crosslinks satisfied by the model), (c) the convergence of the sampling, and (d) a clear definition of what "complete" means in this context (e.g., all residues modeled, all subunits included, etc.).
