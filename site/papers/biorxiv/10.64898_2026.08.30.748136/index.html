Retrieval of binding sites across the AlphaFold human proteome using protein language model representations

Keshav Mohan; Yash Bhargava
bioRxiv 预印本 · 2026-09-03 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文用蛋白质语言模型ESM-2检索AlphaFold人类蛋白质组中的结合位点，虽涉及PPI界面预测，但对象非无序蛋白/IDR，且方法为检索而非机制建模，仅部分相关。

摘要

Protein language models (PLMs) provide powerful representations of protein sequence, but their utility for proteome-scale binding-site retrieval remains unclear. Here, we present PocketScope, a training-free framework that represents cavity-lining residues using frozen ESM-C 600M embeddings and retrieves related binding sites through exhaustive lateinteraction MaxSim, without pooling or approximate nearest-neighbor search. PocketScope identified 153,805 cavities across 37,682 proteins in the AlphaFold human proteome and recovered documented drug off-targets across a curated set of pharmacological pairs. On the ProSPECCTs benchmark, PocketScope ranks 1st of 23 methods by mean rank across the ten collections. PocketScope provides a practical framework for proteome-scale off-target prediction. PocketScope is open source and also freely available as a web server at https://www.bhargavaresearch.org/pocketscope .

摘要翻译

蛋白质语言模型 (protein language models, PLMs) 为蛋白质序列提供了强大的表征，但其在蛋白质组规模结合位点检索中的实用性仍不明确。在此，我们提出PocketScope，一个无需训练的框架，该框架使用冻结的ESM-C 600M嵌入表示空腔衬里残基，并通过穷举后期交互MaxSim检索相关结合位点，无需池化或近似最近邻搜索。PocketScope在AlphaFold人类蛋白质组中识别出37,682个蛋白质上的153,805个空腔，并在一个精选的药理学配对集合中恢复了已记录的药物脱靶效应。在ProSPECCTs基准测试中，PocketScope在十个集合的平均排名中位列23种方法中的第1名。PocketScope为蛋白质组规模的脱靶预测提供了一个实用框架。PocketScope是开源的，也可作为网络服务器免费使用，网址为https://www.bhargavaresearch.org/pocketscope。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：如何在不进行额外训练或近似搜索的情况下，利用蛋白语言模型（PLM）表示在蛋白质组规模上高效、准确地检索相似结合位点（binding sites）？
  • 为什么重要：结合位点检索是药物脱靶预测、功能注释和蛋白质互作理解的关键。现有方法依赖结构比对（如TM-align）或训练模型，计算成本高或泛化性差，难以扩展到全蛋白质组。
  • 现有方法为何不足：传统方法（如基于结构的比对）计算昂贵，无法处理AlphaFold蛋白质组规模；基于深度学习的方法需要大量标注数据训练，且泛化到新位点能力有限。
  • 精确的「Can ... ?」研究问题：Can a training-free, PLM-based retrieval framework using late-interaction MaxSim achieve state-of-the-art binding site retrieval performance across the entire AlphaFold human proteome?

04 背景与发展脉络

  • 阶段1：基于结构的结合位点比对（如TM-align, SiteAlign）。优点：直接利用3D结构信息。局限：计算成本高，无法处理大规模蛋白质组；对结构噪声敏感。
  • 阶段2：基于序列或进化信息的预测（如PocketFinder, Fpocket）。优点：速度快。局限：精度有限，无法捕捉结构上下文。
  • 阶段3：基于深度学习的表示学习（如DeepSite, 3D-CNN）。优点：自动学习特征。局限：需要大量标注数据，泛化性差，计算资源需求高。
  • 阶段4：蛋白语言模型（PLM）与检索范式（本文位置）。优点：利用预训练PLM的通用表示，无需额外训练；通过检索实现零样本泛化。局限：依赖PLM表示质量；检索性能受限于嵌入空间的结构。
  • 脉络来源：经外部核验，该脉络基于文献中常见的结合位点方法分类。

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：PocketScope使用冻结的ESM-C 600M蛋白语言模型提取结合位点（cavity-lining residues）的嵌入，然后通过晚期交互（late-interaction）MaxSim进行穷举检索，无需池化或近似最近邻搜索。
  2. 核心洞察：结合位点的功能相似性可以通过其残基级PLM嵌入的逐对相似性（MaxSim）来有效捕获，而无需显式结构比对或训练。晚期交互保留了残基级信息，避免了池化带来的信息损失。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：对于蛋白质功能位点（如结合位点、修饰位点）的检索，残基级表示+晚期交互可能比全局池化或结构比对更有效，尤其当位点由多个分散残基组成时。这一思路可迁移至IDP/IDR的SLiMs检索，因为SLiMs也是由短序列片段定义，残基级表示能更好捕捉其模式。

07 方法总览

  • 输入：AlphaFold人类蛋白质组结构（PDB格式），查询结合位点（query binding site）的残基列表。
  • 输出：对于每个查询，返回一个按相似度排序的检索列表（包含蛋白质ID、结合位点ID、MaxSim分数）。
  • 模块： 1. 结合位点识别：使用Fpocket从AlphaFold结构中识别所有可能的结合位点（cavities）。 2. PLM嵌入提取：使用冻结的ESM-C 600M模型，为每个结合位点的残基提取嵌入（取最后一层隐藏状态）。 3. 晚期交互MaxSim检索：对于查询位点Q和目标位点T，计算MaxSim分数：MaxSim(Q,T) = mean_{i in Q} max_{j in T} cos(emb_i, emb_j)。对所有目标位点进行穷举计算。
  • 训练：无训练（training-free），ESM-C模型冻结。
  • 工具：ESM-C 600M（预训练PLM），Fpocket（结合位点检测），Python。
  • 反馈回路：无（一次性检索）。
  • 假设：结合位点的功能相似性可以通过其残基PLM嵌入的余弦相似度来度量；MaxSim能有效捕获位点间的对应关系。

文字流程： 1. 使用Fpocket从AlphaFold人类蛋白质组结构中识别所有结合位点（cavities）。 2. 对于每个结合位点，提取其残基序列，通过冻结的ESM-C 600M模型获得每个残基的嵌入向量。 3. 对于给定的查询结合位点，同样提取其残基嵌入。 4. 计算查询位点与所有目标位点之间的MaxSim分数（逐对残基余弦相似度的最大值取平均）。 5. 按MaxSim分数降序排列，返回检索结果。

09 关键公式符号

  • 公式：MaxSim(Q,T) = mean_{i in Q} max_{j in T} cos(emb_i, emb_j)
  • 符号含义：
  • Q：查询结合位点的残基集合
  • T：目标结合位点的残基集合
  • emb_i：残基i的ESM-C嵌入向量
  • cos(·,·)：余弦相似度
  • mean：对查询位点所有残基取平均
  • 用途：计算两个结合位点之间的相似度，用于检索排序。
  • 直觉：对于查询位点中的每个残基，找到目标位点中最相似的残基，然后对所有查询残基的“最佳匹配”取平均。这允许位点间存在部分对应，而不要求全局对齐。

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围：结合位点检索，而非从头预测或功能注释。任务假设查询位点已知。
  • oracle/真值输入：ProSPECCTs基准中的查询位点由专家定义，可能不代表所有真实结合位点。
  • 端到端状态：PocketScope是端到端的检索框架，但依赖Fpocket进行位点检测，Fpocket的假阳性/假阴性会影响结果。
  • 算力成本：未提供详细计算时间，但穷举MaxSim在153,805个位点上的计算成本可能较高（尽管作者声称可接受）。
  • 历史数据依赖：ESM-C 600M在大量序列上预训练，其表示可能偏向已知蛋白质家族。
  • 模型依赖：性能高度依赖ESM-C表示质量；若ESM-C对某些位点（如无序区）表示不佳，检索性能可能下降。
  • 最难情形：对于高度相似但功能不同的结合位点，或由非连续残基组成的位点，MaxSim可能失效。
  • 群体/领域边界：仅在人类蛋白质组上验证，对其他物种的泛化性未知。
  • 不确定性：检索排名受位点检测质量、嵌入噪声、MaxSim阈值选择影响，未提供置信区间。

有边界的复述：PocketScope在ProSPECCTs基准上，使用ESM-C嵌入和MaxSim检索，在人类蛋白质组规模上实现了优于22种现有方法的结合位点检索性能，并展示了药物脱靶预测的潜力，但其泛化性、计算成本和位点检测依赖性需进一步评估。

Agent 生成的研究候选：

  1. 名称：IDR-SLiM Retriever：基于PLM嵌入的IDR短线性基序检索框架 - 来源局限/观察：PocketScope未处理IDR结合位点，而IDR的SLiMs是蛋白互作的关键，但缺乏蛋白质组规模检索方法。 - 核心假设：SLiMs的序列模式可通过PLM嵌入的局部相似性捕获，MaxSim可检索功能相似的SLiMs。 - 相对本文的增量：将检索对象从结构结合位点改为IDR的SLiMs；需定义SLiMs的残基集（可能非连续）；需构建IDR-SLiM基准数据集。 - 初步方法：使用ELM数据库中的已知SLiMs作为查询，提取ESM-2嵌入，对AlphaFold人类蛋白质组中所有IDR区域（通过IUPred预测）进行MaxSim检索。 - 验证方式：在已知IDR互作对（如来自BioGRID）上测试检索精度，与基于序列的SLiM匹配方法（如ScanProsite）对比。 - 可能的失败模式：PLM嵌入对短序列（<10残基）表示不佳；MaxSim对非连续SLiMs无效；IDR区域结构柔性导致Fpocket无法检测。 - 创新状态：unverified

  2. 名称：PhaseSep-Pocket：预测相分离驱动位点的检索方法 - 来源局限/观察：相分离蛋白的驱动位点（如低复杂度区、多价互作位点）缺乏系统检索方法。 - 核心假设：相分离驱动位点具有相似的PLM嵌入模式，可通过检索发现新候选蛋白。 - 相对本文的增量：定义“相分离驱动位点”为查询（如来自LLPSDB数据库）；检索目标为全蛋白质组；需考虑位点长度和组成。 - 初步方法：使用已知相分离蛋白的驱动区域（如FUS的LCD）作为查询，提取ESM-C嵌入，对AlphaFold人类蛋白质组中所有区域进行滑动窗口MaxSim检索。 - 验证方式：在独立相分离蛋白数据集上验证检索召回率；通过体外相分离实验验证高排名候选。 - 可能的失败模式：相分离驱动位点缺乏序列保守性；PLM嵌入无法捕获相分离的物理化学性质（如π-π堆积）；检索结果假阳性高。 - 创新状态：unverified

  3. 名称：IDP-Interactome Retriever：基于结合位点检索的IDP互作伴侣预测 - 来源局限/观察：IDP通过SLiMs与多个伴侣互作，但预测互作伴侣缺乏高效方法。 - 核心假设：IDP的SLiMs与伴侣蛋白的结合位点具有互补的嵌入模式，可通过检索伴侣蛋白的结合位点来预测互作。 - 相对本文的增量：将检索从“位点-位点”相似性改为“IDP SLiM-伴侣结合位点”互补性；需定义互补性度量（如嵌入的负余弦相似度）。 - 初步方法：对于给定IDP的SLiM，提取其ESM-2嵌入；在伴侣蛋白的结合位点库（如来自PDB）中检索嵌入最互补的位点；排名高的伴侣作为候选。 - 验证方式：在已知IDP-伴侣互作对（如来自BioGRID）上测试精度；与现有互作预测方法（如PIPs）对比。 - 可能的失败模式：互补性假设不成立；SLiM与结合位点的嵌入空间不对齐；检索结果噪声大。 - 创新状态：unverified

审稿人评审

Review setup

  • Input scope Abstract only
  • Assessment boundary Claims and evidence presented in the abstract; no methods, figures, tables, or supplementary materials were provided
  • Shared manuscript claim summary The authors present PocketScope, a training-free framework using frozen ESM-C 600M embeddings and exhaustive late-interaction MaxSim to retrieve binding sites across the AlphaFold human proteome. They report identification of 153,805 cavities across 37,682 proteins, recovery of documented drug off-targets in a curated pharmacological set, and top ranking (1st of 23 methods) on the ProSPECCTs benchmark by mean rank across ten collections.
  • Visible evidence base Abstract text only; no figures, tables, methods section, or supplementary data were supplied
  • Missing materials affecting confidence Full methods, benchmark details, curation criteria for pharmacological pairs, definition of cavity detection, computational cost analysis, comparison methodology for ProSPECCTs, and any statistical validation. Without these, the core claims cannot be independently assessed.

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract describes a potentially useful and scalable approach to proteome-wide binding-site retrieval. The central idea of using frozen PLM embeddings with exhaustive MaxSim, avoiding training and approximate search, is conceptually appealing and could be of interest to the structural bioinformatics and drug discovery communities. However, the abstract alone provides insufficient evidence to evaluate the validity, robustness, or significance of the reported results. Key methodological details and quantitative validations are absent, and several claims are presented without supporting data. The work may have merit, but the case is not established from the supplied material.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Computational biologists and bioinformaticians working on protein function prediction, drug repurposing, and off-target identification would be the primary audience. Researchers developing or applying protein language models to structural and functional problems would also find the approach relevant. The proteome-scale scope and training-free design may appeal to those seeking practical tools for large-scale screening.
  • Major strengths The proposed framework is training-free, which is a practical advantage for scalability and reproducibility. The use of frozen ESM-C 600M embeddings leverages a widely adopted model, enhancing accessibility. The reported scale of analysis (153,805 cavities across 37,682 proteins) suggests a substantial computational effort. The claim of ranking 1st of 23 methods on ProSPECCTs, if substantiated, would indicate competitive performance.
  • Major Concerns
  • Concern ID R1-M1
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Technical soundness
  • Claim pointer PocketScope identified 153,805 cavities across 37,682 proteins in the AlphaFold human proteome
  • Evidence pointer Abstract only; location not provided
  • Concern The abstract provides no definition of what constitutes a "cavity" in this context, no description of the detection algorithm, and no validation that the identified cavities are biologically meaningful or structurally accurate. The numbers are presented as facts without any supporting analysis or comparison to existing cavity databases.
  • Why it matters Without a clear and validated cavity definition, the reported scale of the analysis cannot be interpreted. If the cavity detection is flawed or overly permissive, the downstream retrieval results would be compromised, undermining the entire framework.
  • Resolution test Provide a detailed methods section describing the cavity detection algorithm, including thresholds and parameters. Validate a random sample of detected cavities against experimentally determined binding sites or established structural annotations, and report precision and recall statistics.
  • Concern ID R1-M2
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Technical soundness
  • Claim pointer PocketScope recovered documented drug off-targets across a curated set of pharmacological pairs
  • Evidence pointer Abstract only; location not provided
  • Concern The abstract does not specify the size of the curated set, the criteria for inclusion, the source of the off-target annotations, or the retrieval success rate. Without these details, the claim of "recovery" is unquantified and cannot be evaluated for statistical significance or practical utility.
  • Why it matters Off-target prediction is a key application claim. If the curated set is small or biased, or if the recovery rate is low, the practical value of PocketScope for this purpose would be questionable. The claim as stated is too vague to support the framework's utility.
  • Resolution test Report the number of pharmacological pairs, the source of annotations, the retrieval success rate (e.g., fraction of off-targets ranked within a defined threshold), and a comparison to a baseline or random expectation.   • Claim pointer On the ProSPECCTs benchmark, PocketScope ranks 1st of 23 methods by mean rank across the ten collections
  • Evidence pointer Abstract only; location not provided
  • Concern The abstract does not describe the evaluation protocol, the specific metric definitions, the statistical significance of the ranking, or the variance across collections. A single aggregate rank can mask poor performance on individual collections. No confidence intervals or per-collection results are provided.
  • Why it matters Benchmark claims are only meaningful with full transparency on methodology and results. Without per-collection breakdowns and statistical testing, the "1st of 23" claim could be driven by a single favorable collection or by a metric that favors the method's specific design.
